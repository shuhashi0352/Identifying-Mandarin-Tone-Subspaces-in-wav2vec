Important conceptual point

Because your goal is likely:

detect tone-shape structure

you do not need ultra-high temporal resolution.

Mandarin tones are broad contour patterns:
	•	high level
	•	rising
	•	dipping
	•	falling

These can often be captured with ~10 normalized points.
