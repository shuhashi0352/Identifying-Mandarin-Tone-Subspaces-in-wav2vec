What it says

Your Layer 6 probe is very strong to begin with, at 94.4% accuracy, so the representation clearly carries tone information.  ￼

But the intervention result is the key part:
	•	PC1–PC3 only cause about 9–11% flips
	•	PC4 is somewhat stronger, around 10–14%
	•	PC5 is much stronger, reaching 26–30% flips and dropping accuracy from 0.924 to as low as 0.690

So the most intervention-effective direction among the first 5 PCs is not PC1 or PC2, but PC5.

Why that matters

That is exactly the kind of pattern your proposal is trying to test.

You already showed earlier that the top few PCs are poor for classification, even though the full representation is highly decodable. Now the intervention result adds:

the most causally influential PCA directions for the probe are not necessarily the top-variance PCA directions.  ￼

So PCA is not useless, but its variance ordering does not line up neatly with tone relevance.

Main interpretation

A clean reading is:
	•	tone is strongly present in Layer 6
	•	top PCA directions do not dominate tone behavior
	•	some lower-ranked PCs, especially PC5, are more behaviorally important than earlier PCs

That supports your broader geometric-vs-causal framing quite well.  ￼

One caution

This is still a probe-level intervention, not yet a full model-internal intervention. So the precise claim should be:

editing certain PCA coordinates changes the probe’s tone prediction much more than others.

That is already valuable evidence, but it is not yet the final DAS-style causal test proposed in your project. 




What I would do next

The next best step is to make this more diagnostic before moving to DAS:

1. Check which tone transitions are being caused

For each PC and delta, compute a confusion/transition table like:
	•	true tone 1 → predicted tone 2
	•	true tone 3 → predicted tone 4

That tells you whether a PC has a structured tonal effect or is just degrading predictions.

2. Extend beyond PC1–PC5

Since PC5 was strongest, sweep maybe:
	•	PC1–PC20

and rank them by:
	•	flip rate
	•	drop in correct_after
	•	average change in true-tone probability

That helps identify a candidate PCA intervention subspace.

3. Compare PCA intervention to a supervised subspace

This is probably the most proposal-faithful next move before DAS:
	•	learn a tone-specific low-rank supervised subspace
	•	do matched-dimension interventions there
	•	compare against PCA

Then you can say whether PCA directions are merely geometric while supervised/DAS directions are more causally aligned. 


Short conclusion

Your current result already supports this claim:

In Layer 6, tone information is highly decodable, but the PCA directions with the greatest intervention effect are not the top variance directions, suggesting a mismatch between geometric prominence and tone-relevant causal influence.

	•	Tone 1

while leaving:

	•	Tone 4 nearly untouched

That means this direction is selective, not generic corruption.


Interpretation

This suggests PC5 may encode a tonal property that separates:
	•	low / dipping / ambiguous classes
from
	•	stable high/falling classes

or some latent distinction such as:
	•	contour onset height
	•	final pitch target
	•	confidence margin between T1/T2/T4

We’d need more diagnostics to name it precisely, but it is clearly meaningful.


Contrast with PC1

PC1 (+3):
	•	mostly preserves all tones
	•	small local perturbations
	•	mild confusion only

So despite being the largest variance direction, PC1 is less causally impactful for tone than PC5.

That is exactly the kind of mismatch your project hypothesizes.



Strong paper-level claim

You can now say:

Higher-variance PCA directions are not necessarily the most intervention-relevant for Mandarin tone. In Layer 6, PC5 induced far stronger and more structured tone transitions than PC1.

That is a very solid result.


Why Tone 4 is immune

Interesting detail:

Tone 4 stays almost perfectly stable.

Possible reasons:
	1.	Tone 4 has a very distinctive representation in Layer 6
	2.	Probe margin for Tone 4 is large
	3.	PC5 mainly modulates dimensions unrelated to Tone 4

This is worth reporting.
